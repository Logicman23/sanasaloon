WEBSITE PHOTOS - Sana's Beauty Saloon
=====================================

Put your photos in these folders using EXACTLY these file names (lowercase, .jpg).
A photo appears on the website as soon as it is added and the site is redeployed.
Until then, that spot shows the normal design, so nothing looks broken.

Keep each photo under about 500 KB if you can (the website resizes them automatically).

  slide-1.jpg          Slide 1 - "Sana's Beauty Saloon" (the salon, team or a signature look)
  slide-2.jpg          Slide 2 - "Bridal Season" (a bride)
  slide-3.jpg          Slide 3 - "Healthy hair, glowing skin" (hair or facial work)
  On phones the photo sits behind the text, so faces near the top work best.
